# **Carter Website – Review Feedback for Victor**

Oct 14, 2026 · @Oakvale Learning

This summarises Dr. Funke's page-by-page review of the Carter Consulting website. The aim is to get the site live this week so it can be shared with the UCL team. Case studies will continue to be updated in the background after launch, so the current case study content is fine for now.

Items marked **(content coming)** depend on copy or assets the team will send separately.

## **1\. Site-wide changes**

These apply across every page.

•         **Images:** Replace the headline and background image:s throughout. Use one consistent Nigerian/African theme (the Nigerian landmarthe image used herender headings is too small almost everywhere. Size it up so it is easy to read.  
•         **Decorative numbering:** Remove numbers that don't lead anywhere (e.g. "05", "01.1", "01/03"). Keep numbering only where the placement genuinely works.  
•         **Fonts:** Too many font styles and sizes appear in single sections. Keep sizes proportional and limit each area to one or two styles. Review the italic heading font, which looks odd in several places.  
•         **Cut-off letters:** In the scrolling text lines, letters with descenders (g, y, p) are clipped. Fix the row height wherever this happens, including the footer section.  
•         **Line breaks:** When a statement runs over two lines, the bottom line should be the longer one. No single words hanging on their own line.  
•         **Title Case:** Use Title Case for headings, labels and role titles, applied consistently.  
•         **Greens:** Several different shades of green are in use. Keep them consistent across the site.  
•         **Footer call-to-action section** ("The right expertise for the decision ahead"): this appears on several pages. Apply the fixes listed under the Homepage everywhere it appears, and change its background image.  
•         **Keep as is:** The logo and its font, the Carter watermark, and the way the company registration number is shown. Dr. Funke loves these; they look clean and classy.
